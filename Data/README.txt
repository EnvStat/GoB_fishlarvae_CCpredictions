Whitefish and vendace larval dataset was produced by Lari Veneranta and Richard Hudd at the Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke) in years 2009-2011. Commercial fishery statistics were obtained from the Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke), Luke Statistics Database and were aggregated for the present study. The Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke) is the owner and rights holder of the larval fish and commercial fishery datasets. When reusing the data, please cite the dataset using the citation provided in this metadata record and acknowledge the Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke).
